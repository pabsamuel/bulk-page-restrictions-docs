## Getting started

Open a page, select **•••** → **Bulk restrictions for this page tree**, or go to **Space settings → Integrations → Bulk Page Restrictions**. Full steps: [user guide](guide.html).

## Before you write

- Your site URL (`yoursite.atlassian.net`)
- The top page you chose and what you asked the app to do
- The error shown in the app, or the CSV report of the change

## Known limits

- Confluence requires the person who changes a restriction to stay on it, so you are added to restrictions the app writes for you.
- Pages with 200 or more people or groups in one restriction are left alone.
- Pages and spaces you can't see are not included, in changes or in the audit report.
- Keep the app window open while a change runs; if it closes, resume it from **History**.
- Confluence limits how many requests apps can make per hour. Very large trees (thousands of pages) may pause with a "limiting requests" message; resume after a few minutes.
- Change records are deleted **30 days** after a change was started.
- Page restrictions require a paid Confluence plan (Standard or above).

## Updates

Releases are published through the Atlassian Marketplace with release notes. Minor updates install automatically; an update that needs new permissions must be approved by a Confluence administrator.
